🏥 Lung Cancer Prediction and SDG-3 Impact

AI for Good: Supporting Sustainable Development Goal 3 – Good Health and Well-being

📌 Why This Matters:

Lung cancer is one of the most aggressive and fatal diseases, especially when not detected early.

  • 🔬 Uses advanced machine learning algorithms (Random Forest, XGBoost, etc.).
  • 🧠 Built using real patient data with 16 health-related features.
  • 🤖 Deployed for web and Raspberry Pi for real-time screening.

🌍 SDG-3 Goals Aligned:

  • 🚑 Goal 3.4 aims to reduce premature mortality from NCDs by one-third by 2030.
  • 🩺 Lung cancer accounts for 1 in 5 cancer deaths globally.
  • 🧭 This app empowers frontline health workers and educators.
🎯 Contributes directly to SDG Target 3.4 and 3.d.
